# Preserve source video characteristics

Video labelling prioritises the source's container, timing, dimensions, audio, subtitles and colour characteristics over normalising every Labelled Copy to one publishing format. Incorporating the Label necessarily re-encodes the video, but any additional conversion or loss that cannot be avoided requires a user decision; HDR must not silently become SDR. This accepts a broader capability-checking and validation surface in exchange for preserving the user's publishing choices and avoiding unexpected changes beyond the requested Label.
