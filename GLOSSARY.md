# AI Content Labelling

Helps people who produce or publish content with coding agents apply visible EU disclosure labels to AI-generated or AI-modified content, following Article 50(4) of the AI Act and Section 2 of the Code of Practice on transparency of AI-generated content.

## Language

### Disclosure

**Label**:
A visible, human-perceivable disclosure attached to a piece of content stating that AI generated or modified it.
_Avoid_: Mark, tag, watermark, badge

**Existing Label**:
A Label already present in the content that this plugin didn't apply, such as a disclaimer line, an "AI generated" text in an image, or a site's own AI notice.
_Avoid_: Badge, disclaimer (alone), pre-existing marking

**Marking**:
A machine-readable signal (metadata, watermark, fingerprint) embedded in content to identify it as AI-generated. Primarily the provider's duty; distinct from a Label.
_Avoid_: Label, watermark (as a synonym for the whole concept)

**EU Icon**:
One of the official Commission icons used as the core of a Label. Comes in three Icon Variants and four Icon Colours.
_Avoid_: Logo, badge, sticker

**Icon Variant**:
Which EU Icon to use: **Basic** ("AI"), **Fully AI-Generated** ("AI GENERATED") or **Partially AI-Modified** ("AI MODIFIED").
_Avoid_: Type, level

**Icon Colour**:
Black, White, Black 50% or White 50%. The plugin applies Black and White only; the 50% ones blend into the picture, so their contrast can't be judged.
_Avoid_: Theme, style

**Labelled Copy**:
A new image or video file carrying the visible Label within its content, derived from an original that stays untouched.
_Avoid_: Output, watermarked image, watermarked video

**Creative Work**:
An image or video the user presents as artistic, satirical or fictional, whose creative character informs how the Label is presented.
_Avoid_: Art, entertainment

### Roles

**Provider**:
The organisation that builds and offers the AI system that generated the content.
_Avoid_: Vendor, model
